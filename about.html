About me

Engineer by training,
data scientist by practice.

I build ML models and data pipelines that solve real problems. Here's the story of how I got here.

I'm Jose Quiroga, a Data Scientist currently working at DataMesh Group in Sydney. My path into data science wasn't a straight line — it started with a BSc in Mechatronics Engineering at ITESM in Mexico (with a study abroad year at the Polytechnic University of Milan), which gave me a deep appreciation for how physical systems work and how data can make them smarter.

From there I moved into automation engineering at Ford Motor Company in Hermosillo, where I used real-time data to hit production targets and reduce equipment downtime. That experience showed me how impactful data could be at scale — and pushed me to go deeper. I moved to Sydney to pursue an MSc in Data Science and Innovation at UTS, alongside an MEng in AI and IoT, and haven't looked back since.

Today I work across the full data science lifecycle — from designing ELT pipelines on GCP to building and deploying predictive models that directly influence business decisions. I've delivered a 47% reduction in cloud infrastructure costs and a 90% reduction in critical data errors in my current role. I care about work that is technically rigorous and genuinely useful.

I'm fluent in Spanish (native), English, and Italian (B2), and I've lived and worked across three countries. That international perspective shapes how I approach problems and how I communicate across teams.
